Agent HQ

Privacy policy

Agent HQ is a personal dashboard run by Omar Jaradat for his own YouTube and TikTok accounts. It is not offered to anyone else.

When Omar connects his own YouTube or TikTok account, Agent HQ reads only his channel and video statistics (views, likes, subscribers, watch time). It uses read-only access and never posts, edits, or deletes anything.

The sign-in tokens are stored as encrypted GitHub Actions secrets. The statistics are shown on this site. No data is sold or shared with anyone, and no data from other people's accounts is collected.

To stop access at any time, remove "Agent HQ" from your Google account's third-party connections or your TikTok app permissions.
