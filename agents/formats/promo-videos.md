# LinkedAI promo videos (Promo Studio)

Written by `agents/gemini/promo_studio.py build` Mon/Wed/Fri from the research the hourly chunks saved on the server.
Same script format as tiktok-scripts.md + promo-scripts.md. On top of that:

## Feel
- It's Omar's normal sit-down video where he happens to pass along a tool he found. Same voice as his best organic videos: regular guy, calm, direct, a little dry. Never an ad read, never hype words.
- Borrow the FORMAT of the model video (the structure, the tension, the reveal), not its words.
- The tool shows up as proof, not as the topic: the problem (LinkedIn feels pointless, cold messages get ignored, every resume gets rejected, profile looks dead) is the topic.

## Formats that fit a sit-down creator
- **I found a tool**: "I found a Chrome extension that..." then one feature doing one thing on screen.
- **Problem then solution**: the frustration first (5-10s), then the fix on screen.
- **Screen demo**: he talks over a screen recording (green-screen style); the recording carries the video.
- **Skit / POV**: two characters he plays himself, or "POV: you finally message the recruiter right".
- **Reaction**: he reacts to what LinkedAI wrote ("wait, it found that we both worked retail?").
- **Before vs after**: the message he used to send vs the one LinkedAI writes.

## Screen recordings ("shots")
Concrete, filmable steps, e.g. "Open a recruiter's LinkedIn profile, click the LinkedAI icon, pick Friendly tone, let the common-ground message appear". Only features listed in SYSTEM_MAP.md's LinkedAI section. 3-5 per video, each tied to a scene.

## Never
- "I built", "my app", "my extension", "we made".
- Invented results, numbers, testimonials or users. Use [fill in: your real result].
- Links or view counts the writer makes up. The code fills every link from the research library.
